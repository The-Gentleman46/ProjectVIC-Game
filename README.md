This is a project that is being created for the non-profit ProjectVIC (website: projectvic.org)

These games will be integrated into their website as a resource for law enforcement individuals. 
This project is going to try to incorporate the following games:
- Pong (Single Player & Co-Op)
- Checkers (Single Player & Co-Op)
- Frogger (Single Player & Co-Op)
- Cybersweeper
- Drawing
- Co-Op Puzzle Game (Name TBD)
- Hole Game 

Rules of each Game as listed above:

Pong:
Rules:
	- Hit the ball across the screen to the opposing player/NPC side.
	- Try to hit the ball past your opponent's barrier.
	- Earn points when your opponent fails to return the ball to your side of the screen.
	- First to 11 points wins!

Controls:
	- Use the arrow keys or WASD to move your barrier up and down.
	- If you are in multiplayer mode, Player 1 uses WASD, and Player 2 uses the arrow keys.
	
Checkers:
Rules:
	- Red pieces have the first move. Blue pieces move second. 
	- Pieces can only move forward to the square that is diagonal from where they are placed.
	- Pieces can only move one square at a time.
	- To capture an opponent's piece, the player must hop over their opponent's piece if there is an empty square immediately beyond the piece.
	- To crown a piece, you must make it to the opposite side of the board. Once it reaches the other side, the piece is crowned and can now move along any diagonal.
	
Controls: 
	- To select the piece that you would like to move, left-click the piece with the mouse.
	- To move the piece from its current location, left-click the diagonal direction that is available.
	
Frogger:
Rules:
	- Move across the obstacles to get to the other side of the screen.
	- Hop across the road avoiding the cars (DON'T GET SQUISHED)
	- Next, hop across the creek by standing on the lilypads and logs
	- Stay aware of any other animals that may be in the water. Alligators and snakes are looking to make you their next meal if you don't move fast.
	- There will be 5 locations for you to hop into to complete the level, get 5 different frogs into their different locations and you will complete the level.
	
Controls:
	- Use the arrow keys or WASD to move you barrior up and down.
	- If you are in multiplayer mode, Player 1 uses WASD and Player 2 uses the arrow keys.
	
Cybersweeper:
Rules:
	- Look for the infected Files on your computer.
	- Flag the files and uncover all of the none infected/empty space that is left on your hard drive.
	- Clear all the space to win!
	
Controls:
	- Left-click to reveal the type of tile.
	- Right-click to flag a tile as an infected file.
	
Drawing:
Rules: 
	- Be Creative!
	- Make something fun that you can enjoy
	
Controls:
	- Left-click to use the drawing tool
