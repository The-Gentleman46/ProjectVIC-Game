These games will be intigrated into their website as a resorce for law enforcment individuals. 
This project is going to try and incorperate the following games:
- Pong (Single Player & Co-Op)
- Checkers (Single Player & Co-Op)
- Frogger (Single Player & Co-Op)
- Cybersweeper
- Drawing
- Co-Op Puzzle Game (Name TBD)

Rules of each Game as listed above:

Pong:
Rules:
	- Hit the ball across the screen to the oposing player/NPC side.
	- Try to hit the ball past your oponant's barrior.
	- Earn points when your oponant fails to return the ball to your side of the screen.
	- First to 11 points win!

Controls:
	- Use the arrow keys or WASD to move you barrior up and down.
	- If you are in multiplayer mode Player 1 uses WASD and Player 2 uses the arrow keys.
	
Checkers:
Rules:
	- Orange pieces have the first move. Blue pieces move second. 
	- Pieces can only move forward to the square that is diagonal from where they are placed.
	- Pieces can only move one square at a time.
	- To capture an oponant's piece the player must hop over their oponant's piece if there is an empty square immediatly beyond the piece.
	- To crown a piece you mys make it to the oposite side of the board. Apon making it to the other side the piece will be crowned and will now have the ability to move to any diagonal.
	
Controls: 
	- To select the piece that you would like to move left click the piece with the mouse.
	- To move the peice from its curent location left click the diagonal direction that is avalible.
	
Frogger:
Rules:
	- Move across the obsticals in order to get to the other side of the screen.
	- Hop across the road avoiding the cars (DON'T GET SQUISHED)
	- Next hop across the creek by standing on the lilypads and logs
	- Stay aware of any other animals that may be in the water. Alligators and snakes are looking to make you their next meal if you don't move fast.
	- There will be 5 locations for you to hop into to complete the level get 5 different frogs into their different locations and you will complete the level.
	
Controls:
	- Use the arrow keys or WASD to move you barrior up and down.
	- If you are in multiplayer mode Player 1 uses WASD and Player 2 uses the arrow keys.
	
Cybersweeper:
Rules:
	- Look for the infected Files on your computer.
	- Flag the files and uncover all of the none infected/empty space that is left on your hardrive.
	- Clear all the space to win!
	
Controls:
	- Left Click to revial the type of tile.
	- Right Click to Flag a tile as an infected file.

Controls:
